📖 Background & Origins

Our Story

How a passion for wholesome child development and adult wellness shaped Guwahati's premier mind & motion sanctuary.

Why NOHADAYA Was Established

In an era dominated by constant screen exposure, academic pressure, and sedentary routines, students and adults in Guwahati face escalating mental stress, reduced attention spans, and physical fatigue. Children frequently experience math anxiety and illegible handwriting from improper pen control, while adults struggle to find dedicated time for mindful physical practice.

NOHADAYA was established as a purpose-driven studio in Bagharbari to bridge this gap. We unified three proven developmental pillars under one peaceful roof: Yoga for bodily alignment and stress relief, Abacus for speed arithmetic and concentration, and Calligraphy & Handwriting for fine-motor control and creative discipline.

🏘️

Community Served

We serve a vibrant community of school children, teenagers, working professionals, and homemakers across Bagharbari, Satgaon, Panjabari, Six Mile, VIP Road, Bormotoria, Hengrabari, and Guwahati.

We emphasize structured small batches, individual pacing, patient guidance, and active daily practice. We foster a warm, zero-intimidation environment where every student develops authentic confidence.
