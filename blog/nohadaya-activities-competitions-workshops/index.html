Inside NOHADAYA: Competitions, Workshops & Student Achievements

✍️ NOHADAYA Editorial Desk 📅 September 2026 ⏱️ 4 min read

At NOHADAYA Mind & Motion Studio, learning is an active, celebrated community journey. We believe that structured assessments, friendly competitions, and regular workshops are vital for instilling healthy self-confidence and genuine discipline in learners of all ages.

Building Resilience Through Authentic Competition and Skill Milestones

Participation in structured skill assessments gives students a clear sense of progress and purpose. Preparing for an Abacus championship or a Calligraphy portfolio showcase teaches children how to manage performance pressure, organize their practice schedule, and take pride in diligent craftsmanship.

Event Calendar, Assessment Criteria, and Portfolio Reviews

NOHADAYA hosts quarterly level completion assessments, annual felicitations, International Yoga Day community sessions, and handwriting exhibitions. Every student is evaluated according to standardized rubric benchmarks certified under ISO 9001:2015 quality frameworks.

Celebrating Student Growth at NOHADAYA Mind & Motion Studio

Over 500+ students in Bagharbari and greater Guwahati have participated in our programs. Every milestone achieved is recognized with official certificates, medals, and constructive feedback that empowers students to reach their highest potential in both academics and personal wellness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are competition certificates nationally recognized?

Yes. Our Abacus and Calligraphy certificates are issued under NE Green Abacus and NE Unique Calligraphy standards, verified under ISO 9001:2015 quality management.

Do all students have to participate in competitions?

Competitions are encouraged but voluntary. Students can also progress steadily through our core level-by-level curriculum at their own comfortable pace.
