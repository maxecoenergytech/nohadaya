Why Pen Ergonomics Matter for School Students

Holding a heavy or slippery pen creates excessive finger strain, leading to shaky strokes and slow exam handwriting. Our instructors recommend starting with lightweight chisel-nib or dual-brush pens paired with ergonomic silicone grip correctors.
