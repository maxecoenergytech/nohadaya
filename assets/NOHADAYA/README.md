## 6. What NOT To Do (Quality & Integrity Checklist)

To ensure brand prestige, authentic student trust, and sustainable white-hat SEO rankings:

- ❌ **NO Keyword Stuffing:** Write natural, informative prose for students and parents.
- ❌ **NO Fake Reviews or Testimonials:** Display only genuine, verifiable student and parent feedback.
- ❌ **NO Fake Awards or Expired Certifications:** Display only active accreditations (ISO 9001:2015, NE Green Abacus, NE Unique Calligraphy).
- ❌ **NO Duplicate Location Pages:** Consolidate local authority in Bagharbari with natural catchment references (Satgaon, Panjabari, Six Mile).
- ❌ **NO AI-Generated Generic Spam Blogs:** Publish authentic, locally relevant educational guides.
- ❌ **NO Purchased Backlinks or Spam Directories:** Rely on organic local citations and educational partnerships.
- ❌ **NO Excessive Popups or CTA Clutter:** Clean, distraction-free reading experience with sticky mobile actions.
- ❌ **NO Inconsistent Pricing or Timings:** Maintain a single central source of truth on the Schedule & Fees page.
