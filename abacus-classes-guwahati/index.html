8-Level Syllabus

Abacus Classes in Bagharbari: 8-Level Syllabus

Structured into 8 incremental levels. Every level includes dedicated workbooks, speed test evaluations, and an official course completion certificate.

L1

Foundation & Beads

Fingering technique, Soroban frame anatomy, 1-digit addition/subtraction, direct bead manipulation.

L2

Small Friends

Formula combinations of 5 (+4, +3, +2, +1), two-digit addition, basic mental image visualization.

L3

Big Friends

Formula combinations of 10 (+9 to +1), borrowing and carrying, speed drills with audio dictation.

L4

Mixed Combinations

Complex multidigit operations, negative numbers introduction, rapid flashcard calculation.

L5

Multiplication Mastery

Multiplication tables on abacus, 2-digit by 1-digit, 3-digit by 1-digit mental math expansion.

L6

Division Dynamics

Long division on abacus, remainder mechanics, rapid speed calculation competitions.

L7

Decimal Arithmetic

Decimals, percentages, square roots, multi-row calculation sheets under exam time limits.

L8

Grand Master Anzan

Pure mental arithmetic without tool, state championship readiness, speed champion certificate.
