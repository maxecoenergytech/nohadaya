🧭 Local Guide

How to Reach NOHADAYA

From Satgaon / Army Cantt

Head towards Satgaon Nowapara Jame Masjid. Take Roseland Road directly behind Apple Pie. NOHADAYA is located on the 1st Floor of House No. 422 (2 minutes from Satgaon main junction).

From Panjabari & Bormotoria

Take the Bagharbari connecting road towards Satgaon. Enter Roseland Road near Nowapara Jame Masjid landmark (5–7 minutes drive).

From Six Mile & VIP Road

From Six Mile flyover, take VIP Road towards Panjabari/Bagharbari. Follow Google Maps directly to Roseland Road (8–10 minutes drive). Dedicated two-wheeler and car parking available.

❓ Common Questions

Contact & Studio FAQ

Do I need to book an appointment before visiting?
While walk-ins are welcomed, we strongly recommend calling or messaging us on WhatsApp (+91 70026 04856) in advance so our instructors can dedicate uninterrupted time for your consultation and free trial.
Is parking available at the studio?
Yes, safe roadside and premises parking is available for two-wheelers and cars along Roseland Road, Bagharbari.
What payment methods are accepted for monthly tuition?
We accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), Cash, and Direct Bank Transfers. Monthly tuition is ₹500/month with zero admission lock-ins.
What should I bring to the Free Trial class?
For Yoga: wear comfortable stretchable clothing and bring a personal water bottle (mats are provided). For Abacus/Calligraphy: we provide diagnostic worksheets and stationery for the trial.
