How do I enquire about course fees? ▼
₹500 per month, plus a one-time ₹200 registration fee. This includes 26 classes per month with morning and evening batch options, personal posture guidance by certified instructor Mrs. Noor Nahar Begum, Pranayama breathwork, and meditation. Yoga mats are provided at the studio.
What age group is suitable for Abacus? ▼
The current program is designed for children aged 5–14 years. As structured mind development classes for children, the 8-level NE Green Abacus curriculum develops photographic memory, lightning calculation speed, and school exam confidence.
Is there a trial class? ▼
Yes. NOHADAYA offers a free trial class for new students across all three programs. You can reserve your complimentary pass online by clicking Book Free Trial or messaging our team on WhatsApp at +91 70026 04856.
Do you provide certification? ▼
Yes. Recognized certification is available for applicable Abacus (NE Green Abacus certified level progression & competition trophies) and Calligraphy programs (NE Unique Calligraphy certified course diploma). NOHADAYA is a unit of Mind Castle Mercantile, an ISO 9001:2015 certified institution.
Do you offer morning batches? ▼
Yes. Morning and evening batches are available for selected programs. Morning Yoga classes in Bagharbari run from 6:00 AM – 7:30 AM (Monday to Friday), Morning Abacus runs from 9:00 AM – 10:00 AM, and Morning Calligraphy runs from 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM. Evening batches run from 4:00 PM onwards.
How do Calligraphy and Handwriting classes in Guwahati benefit students? ▼
Our specialized handwriting classes in Guwahati correct improper pen grip, hand fatigue, and messy letterforms to improve exam presentation, while our calligraphy classes for kids in Guwahati teach cursive, Gothic, and modern brush lettering. Fee is ₹500/month with starter kit included.
