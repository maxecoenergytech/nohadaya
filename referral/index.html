Refer a Friend to NOHADAYA Studio

Help your friends and family discover joyful learning and mindful wellness in Guwahati. Transparent guidelines, non-misleading terms, and tuition appreciation credits.

How the Referral System Works

Three simple steps to refer a fellow parent, friend, or neighbor to NOHADAYA.

1

Recommend NOHADAYA

Tell your friend or family member about our Yoga, Abacus, or Calligraphy programs and share your personal experience.

2

They Book a Free Trial

Submit their details below or ask them to mention your name and phone number when they contact the studio for their free demo.

3

Tuition Benefit Applied

Upon their verified admission, both you and the referred student receive a transparent ₹200 fee credit on the next monthly cycle.

📜 Transparent Terms & Conditions (No Misleading Incentives)

  • Eligibility: The referrer must be an active enrolled student or parent at NOHADAYA Studio.
  • New Student Only: The referred individual must be a new student who has not previously attended regular classes at NOHADAYA.
  • Verified Admission: Referral fee credits (₹200 discount per confirmed admission) are credited to your active account upon successful payment of their first regular monthly batch fee.
  • Non-Cash Redemption: Credits apply strictly against monthly tuition renewals or sibling package admissions and cannot be redeemed for cash.
  • No Limit: There is no cap on the number of friends an active student or parent may refer.
