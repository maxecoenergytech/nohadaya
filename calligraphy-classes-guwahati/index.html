Structured Syllabus

Calligraphy Classes for Kids in Guwahati & Adults: Structured Syllabus

Progressive modules certified by NE Unique Calligraphy.

LEVEL 1: BEGINNER

Grip, Strokes & Cursive

  • ✓ Proper posture & pen holding biomechanics
  • ✓ Basic upward (thin) and downward (thick) strokes
  • ✓ Lowercase & uppercase English alphabet formation
  • ✓ Consistent letter height, width & slant angle
  • ✓ Word joining, rhythm & neat exam speed
LEVEL 2: INTERMEDIATE

Broad Nib & Gothic Script

  • ✓ Chisel-edge fountain pen handling (45° angle)
  • ✓ Traditional Gothic / Blackletter calligraphy
  • ✓ Intricate majuscule capitals and dramatic serifs
  • ✓ Spacing, margins, and manuscript layout rules
  • ✓ Ink consistency, nib maintenance & paper choice
LEVEL 3: ADVANCED

Brush Lettering & Art Projects

  • ✓ Modern flexible brush pen pressure manipulation
  • ✓ Elegant flourishes, swashes & embellishments
  • ✓ Blending dual-color inks & shadow effects
  • ✓ Real-world projects: Greeting cards, bookmarks
  • ✓ Capstone project for NE Unique Certification
